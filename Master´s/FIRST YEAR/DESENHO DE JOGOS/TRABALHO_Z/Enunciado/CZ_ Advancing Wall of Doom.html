CZ: Advancing Wall of Doom

This is the final challenge, where you have (almost) free reign on your game.

The only requirement is that the game theme must be:

Advancing Wall of Doom

You are otherwise free to set the mechanics, features, etc.

This is your chance to show-case your mastery of both Godot and Game Design.
image credits: Mœbius

Challenge

Design and Implement a game that:

  • Uses the theme "Advancing Wall of Doom".
  • Respects the course contents.
  • Show-cases:
    • Your technical domain of Godot — make your nodes shine!
    • Your theoretical understanding of Game Design — design a fantastic game!

Resolution

Consider the theme. How is the wall advancing and how is is causing doom? Is it irreversible or you can fix it?

Consider the technical part of this challenge. What, and how, are you going to show-case?

Consider the theoretical part of this challenge. How to highlight the application of what you have learned?

Think about the player's viewpoint

  • What's this game about?
  • How do I play?
  • How do I win?
  • Why do I want to play?
  • What things do I need to do?

Ensure meaningful decisions

  • Consider the players' options.
  • Choices must affect the game's outcome.
  • Look to keep the players' minds busy.

Apply the theme

  • What is the "wall"?
  • How is the "wall" advancing?
  • What is "doom"?
  • How is the "wall" causing "doom"?
  • How does the player deals with "doom" and with the "wall"?

Deliverable

An archive (a zip file) with:

  • A beta prototype of the game (All systems have been implemented and all code is theoretically complete. No major bugs are expected.).
  • A concept document of the game (a pdf file) as outlined in the Concept Document Structure chapter of the course notes.

Optional Game Features

Export the game for the web

Since Godot can export projects for the web this is a nice feature to show your technical skills. Note that there are some issues related with web export.

Casual Variation
